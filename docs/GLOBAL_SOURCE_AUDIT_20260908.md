# Global source expansion — 2026-09-08

Scope: all countries, multiple languages, primary physical opening cohorts.
This is a source audit, not a claim that every country has usable observations.
No country is assigned from a site's language, domain, currency or audience.
The checked facts below are references, not newly admitted production samples.

## Cross-site provenance and exclusions

- [Rocket Monster ME02](https://www.rocketmonster.fr/taux-de-drop-pokemon-mega-evolution-me02/)
  explicitly attributes its study to TCGplayer and links the
  [Phantasmal Flames original](https://www.tcgplayer.com/content/article/Pok%C3%A9mon-TCG-Phantasmal-Flames-Pull-Rates/9abae60d-b7fb-448f-874e-176f78d6a6ca/).
  This is a source relationship, not new French opening samples. The original
  returned no readable body in this check; its counts are not newly admitted.
- [CardTrack methodology on its pull-rate page](https://cardtrack.com/pokemon/pull-rates)
  explicitly says it does not open packs and uses a model. Exclude simulated
  outcomes from observed denominators, even though set/catalog facts may help
  reference matching.
- [Korean ggvalue methodology](https://ggvalue.com/methodology) describes using
  Japanese substitutes when Korean opening data is unavailable. Audit each
  underlying cohort: a Korean-language display does not establish Korean packs.
  Its simulations are not additional observations.
- [DigitalTQ 151](https://www.digitaltq.com/pokemon-151-pull-rates-pokemon-tcg)
  exposes a 700-pack table, but original-session overlap remains unresolved.
  Retain the existing research candidate; do not add a new independent cohort
  for this repeat discovery or merge other sets on that domain into its sample.
- [WhatsThatROI methodology](https://www.whatsthatroi.com/methodology) separates
  measured studies, models and missing data. It is a discovery index, not a
  new opening cohort for every set listed there.

Multilingual searches also covered German, Portuguese, Chinese, Thai, Korean,
Japanese, French and English queries, including South Africa and Australia.
These searches are not exhaustive country audits. Retail listings, Pocket
results and anecdotes lacking validated denominators do not close those gaps.

## Aggregation requirements

Keep original cohort aliases across translations/reprints; do not sum changed
snapshots or partially overlapping video compilations. Exact counts, lower
bounds and title claims stay separate. Pool only compatible set, language,
product and sampling definitions after independence review. Large samples can
reduce random sampling error; they do not remove selective publication or
product-selection bias. Absence of a country's evidence means unknown, not zero.

The updated seed will enter the existing global workflow after reviewed merge.
This file does not enable a collector, change Supabase, or increase live totals.
