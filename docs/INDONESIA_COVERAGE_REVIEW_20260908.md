## Access, rights and limits

The exact public article and [robots](https://bikuhime.wordpress.com/robots.txt)
were reviewed from MAM. The article path is not disallowed. No login, bypass,
proxy, video/image request or bulk discovery crawl is needed.
[WordPress terms](https://wordpress.com/tos/) retain publisher ownership and
prohibit rights infringement and excessive infrastructure load. The platform's
license/reblog provisions are not treated as a third-party republication grant.
No separate publisher terms page was found. The approved extraction is limited
to minimum noncreative facts and short product/pack labels, not article prose,
images, personal details, or the whole results table.

The adapter validates table column identity, the complete first-box totals,
publication metadata, title and exact excerpt hash in memory. Only the short
fact excerpt and coverage metadata may be retained for 730 days. Raw HTML and
media are never persisted. Two HTTP pages maximum (robots plus article), one
item, concurrency one, 30-second spacing and a daily cache/schedule bound apply.
Only `bikuhime.wordpress.com` is enabled, not other WordPress sites.
Robots denial, changed structure/evidence, rights complaints or ownership drift
stop collection pending review; disable the exact source policy as kill switch.

The inherited `statistics_eligible_default=true` is a coverage admission gate,
not probability eligibility. This contract contains no numerator or rate metric.
