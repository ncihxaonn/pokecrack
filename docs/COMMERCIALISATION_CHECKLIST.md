# Commercialisation checklist

This is a pre-commercial **checklist only**, not legal advice, clearance, a business plan or commercial implementation. The MVP contains no Stripe, subscription, advertising, affiliate, sponsored-listing or commercial/public paid-API product code.

Before any monetisation or public commercial launch, obtain accountable owner/sign-off for every applicable item:

## Required review items

- [ ] **Vercel plan:** confirm Hobby remains permitted for the intended commercial traffic, team and deployment model; price the correct tier.
- [ ] **Supabase plan:** confirm project limits, backups, support, egress and commercial-use requirements; price the correct tier.
- [ ] **Data-source terms:** perform a written source-by-source review of website/API terms, robots, redistribution, retention, derived-data and commercial-use rights.
- [ ] **Social-platform API terms:** separately review every authenticated adapter, account-consent model, API/platform automation rule and revocation path.
- [ ] **Pokémon name, card art and trademarks:** obtain copyright, database-rights and trademark advice for names, logos, card images, video/transcript excerpts and screenshots.
- [ ] **Pokecrack name:** perform trademark, company-name and domain clearance; do not treat this working name or a domain search as legal clearance.
- [ ] **Privacy policy:** document lawful basis, data inventory, subprocessors, cross-border transfers, cookies/analytics and data-subject processes.
- [ ] **Data-retention policy:** define retention/deletion schedules, takedown/correction handling and evidence-storage limits.
- [ ] **Misleading-promotion risk:** independently review every claim, signal label and campaign so observations cannot be read as guarantees, buying advice, a “hot pack/store” claim or gambling encouragement.
- [ ] **Stripe:** assess account eligibility, payment security, billing architecture and regional availability before writing an integration.
- [ ] **Tax:** obtain entity, GST/VAT/sales-tax, invoicing and accounting advice for every target jurisdiction.
- [ ] **Refund policy:** define cancellation, refund, chargeback and support obligations before accepting money.
- [ ] **Terms of service:** draft and review service scope, methodology limitations, acceptable use, liability, suspension, complaints and dispute terms.

## Additional go/no-go review

- [ ] Independent counsel review of affiliation, consumer, advertising, competition, gambling/loot-box, age/child-safety and regional laws.
- [ ] Independent statistical review, bias/coverage disclosure, versioned reproducibility and correction policy.
- [ ] Accessibility, security, abuse, takedown, complaints and editorial/advertising-separation policies.
- [ ] Production threat model, penetration review, least privilege, secret management, SBOM/dependency/image pinning and incident response.
- [ ] Capacity/SLO/on-call/monitoring, backup/restore/RTO/RPO, disaster recovery and vendor exit.
- [ ] Provider contracts, billing and budget controls for AI, database, hosting, email and APIs.
- [ ] Explicit sponsored/affiliate-influence policy; payment must never alter source eligibility or signal labels.

## Go/no-go

Record reviewer, evidence, date, expiry/re-review trigger and residual risk for every item. Any unresolved trademark, source commercial-right, privacy, methodology or security blocker is a no-go. After approval, commercial features require a separate scoped design, threat review, tests and release—not a checkbox change to this free MVP.
