## Pokémon GO

explicitly states 38 opened packs from three ETBs and one Radiant Eevee collection.
It reports 12 packs with an art, radiant or peelable Ditto card, and 26 without.

The source category is not a normalized SIR/IR rarity definition. No opening
country, product language or opening date has been verified. The page exposes a
2025-11-29 modification date, which does not establish the opening date.

## Crown Zenith — quarantine, not admission

The [original report](https://www.codedyellow.com/what-is-the-crown-zenith-pull-rate/)
states 50 opened packs from five ETBs. Its results section says 28 packs had
art/Galarian Gallery cards and 22 did not. Its conclusion says 22 packs had art
cards and reports 27 art cards overall. A category difference may explain some
of this, but it is not resolved by the available prose. Do not choose one number
or silently treat the two sections as equivalent standardized rarity definitions.

Preserve both section-level claims with the same original cohort and URL. The
ledger quarantines the component until counts and definitions are reconciled;
two rows are not two openings. In particular, 27 cards / 50 packs is card yield,
not a 54% chance of a pack containing at least one art card.

The same publisher also has a [Crown Zenith weight experiment](https://www.codedyellow.com/crown-zenith-pack-weight/),
which MAM verified also reports 50 packs from five ETBs.
Same publisher/set is insufficient to merge cohorts or establish independence.
Do not add that experiment's count without auditing original-session overlap.
Neither Coded Yellow cohort is enabled for production collection by this change.
