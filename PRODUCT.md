# Pokecrack product context

## What it is

Pokecrack is a free, personal, experimental and unofficial research dashboard for observed Pokémon TCG pack-opening activity. It helps people inspect published aggregate evidence across sets, products, regions, retailers and visible batches without presenting observations as predictions.

## Primary user job

Answer: “What has been observed, how much evidence supports it, and how cautiously should I interpret it?”

## Product principles

- Evidence before excitement: show sample size, source coverage, freshness and uncertainty beside every signal.
- Observation, not prediction: never imply that a region, retailer, batch or product guarantees a future result.
- Public clarity: the first screen should make the map, current scope and strongest aggregate facts understandable at a glance.
- Progressive detail: overview first; methodology, exact rows and caveats remain one step away.
- Honest state: demo, live, withheld, unavailable and insufficient states must be visually and semantically distinct.

## Current scope

- English-language observations of physical Booster Boxes, ETBs and Booster Bundles.
- Current published regional coverage is Australia-only.
- The online application is a tested demo-mode Web deployment, not an unattended live collection system.
- Worldwide coverage and a global map are a planned follow-up, not part of this UI refinement.

## Out of scope

- Buying advice, “hot pack” prediction or store-luck rankings.
- Guaranteed pull rates or claims about individual products.
- Gambling, automated purchasing, paid alerts or public uploads.
